# Reviewer Personalities (Short Descriptions)

## 1) MIT Professor Reviewer (existing)
**Profile:** Academic, methodical, and balanced.
**Personality:** Prioritizes novelty, technical correctness, and community impact; critical but fair, with a strong emphasis on scientific rigor and clarity.

## 2) Industry-Friendly Reviewer
**Profile:** Principal Compiler Engineer focused on practical adoption.
**Personality:** Constructive and improvement-oriented; looks for reasons to accept while giving concrete, high-leverage suggestions that make the paper stronger.

## 3) Industry-Skeptical Reviewer
**Profile:** Distinguished Performance Architect focused on risk and evidence quality.
**Personality:** Adversarial and detail-sensitive; actively searches for weak assumptions, tiny inconsistencies, and under-supported claims to justify rejection unless evidence is strong.
